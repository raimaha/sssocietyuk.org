Our journey

How the Society began

  1. 16 May 2026

    A Standing Committee is formed

    A group of community members came together as a Standing Committee to lay the foundations of the Society. Manoj Pun chaired the Committee and Dr Maha Rai led the drafting of the Society’s Constitution.

  2. 16 August 2026

    Members shape the Constitution

    At the first General Meeting, members reviewed the draft Constitution together, discussing how the Society should be run, how decisions should be made and what it should stand for.

  3. 1 November 2026

    The First General Convention

    Members formally adopted the Constitution and elected the Society’s first Executive Committee, marking the official beginning of Shram Sanskriti Society UK.
