Membership

Membership

Membership is open to persons of Nepalese origin, members of the Nepalese diaspora and any other person with a genuine interest in supporting the Society’s aims.

Annual Membership Article 5.2

£10 per year

The annual membership subscription is initially £10. A reduced or waived rate may be set for hardship Article 5.3.

  • Each Eligible Voting Member has one vote at meetings of the General Assembly Article 5.7
  • A member is an Eligible Voting Member if they are aged 18 or over, their membership is current, and they are not suspended Article 5.6
  • A membership that is not renewed within 60 days of its renewal date shall lapse Article 5.4

Recognised roles Article 5.2

The Society recognises the following roles, which do not create separate classes of membership or voting rights:

  • Founding Member — an honorary recognition for a person who contributed to establishing the Society. Founding Members Register
  • General Volunteer Member — a member who supports the Society through voluntary service, programmes, activities or community engagement.

Rights of members Article 5.5

Every Eligible Voting Member has the right to:

  • attend the AGM, any EGM and any General Convention;
  • take part in discussions and consultations;
  • submit proposals in accordance with the Standing Orders;
  • vote on matters before the General Assembly;
  • stand for election, subject to the eligibility requirements of the office; and
  • receive appropriate information about the Society’s governance, activities and finances.

Responsibilities of members Article 5.8

Members shall:

  • uphold the Constitution and the Society’s lawful objectives;
  • act honestly, respectfully and responsibly;
  • respect the rights and dignity of other members;
  • comply with the Society’s policies;
  • avoid conduct that materially harms the Society or its members; and
  • declare relevant conflicts of interest.

Apply for membership Article 5.4

Membership application

Applications, admission and renewal are overseen by the Vice Chair Coordinator — Membership and Records, in coordination with the General Secretary and Treasurer Article 7.4.

Every applicant shall agree to uphold the Constitution and the lawful objectives of the Society Article 5.1.

Membership records shall be kept securely and processed in accordance with data protection law Article 5.12.

General Volunteer Members Article 7.13

Voluntary service

General Volunteer Members may assist with programmes, fundraising, events, communications and other activities. They have full rights at General Assembly meetings if they are Eligible Voting Members.

Dignity of labour (Shram). All work, manual and intellectual, is honoured equally, and every member contributes according to their ability Article 2.8.
