Membership

Join our community

Membership is open to everyone of Nepalese origin, members of the wider Nepalese diaspora, and anyone who shares our aims and wants to support our work.

Why join

More than a membership

Have your say

Every member has one equal vote. You can attend our Annual General Meeting, put forward proposals and help decide the Society’s direction.

Lead and serve

Members can stand for election to the Executive Committee and take on leadership roles that make a real difference.

Connect and belong

Meet people who share your heritage and values, take part in cultural events, and help pass on Nepal’s traditions to the next generation.

Annual membership

£10 per year
  • One member, one vote — no one has extra votes because of their position
  • Voting rights for members aged 18 and over
  • Invitations to the Annual General Meeting and all general meetings
  • Regular updates on the Society’s activities and finances

A reduced or waived fee may be available for anyone facing financial hardship. Membership is renewed annually.

What we ask of members

Membership is built on mutual respect. We ask every member to:

  • support the Society’s aims and values
  • act honestly, respectfully and responsibly
  • respect the rights and dignity of fellow members
  • follow the Society’s policies
  • declare any conflict of interest

Apply

Join the Society

Complete the form and our membership coordinator will be in touch with your membership form and payment details.

Once your membership is confirmed, you will be welcomed into the Society and kept informed about upcoming meetings, events and volunteering opportunities.

Your details are kept securely and used only to manage your membership.

Volunteer with us

Give your time and skills

Volunteering is at the heart of Shram Sanskriti. In the spirit of Shram, every contribution is valued equally — whether you help set up a hall, design a poster or advise on finance.

Events & programmes

Help plan and run cultural celebrations, community gatherings, workshops and seminars.

Community projects

Support educational, environmental and heritage projects in the UK and Nepal.

Fundraising

Help organise fundraising events and build relationships with supporters and sponsors.

Communications

Lend your skills in writing, design, photography, social media or translation.

Youth & heritage

Help pass on languages, stories and traditions to young people growing up in Britain.

Professional skills

Offer expertise in finance, law, IT, education or health to strengthen the Society.
