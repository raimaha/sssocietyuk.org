Principal aim Article 3.1

Uniting the Nepalese diaspora

The principal aim of the Society is to unite and engage members of the Nepalese diaspora in the United Kingdom through voluntary service, civic responsibility, cultural preservation, community solidarity and the ethical principles of Shram Sanskriti.

Drafting and adoption history Article 18.8

How the Society was established

  1. 16 May 2026

    Standing Committee formed

    The Standing Committee (Interim Coordinating Committee) of SSS UK was formed, under the chairmanship of Manoj Pun. Dr Maha Rai was designated Constitution Coordinator.

  2. 16 August 2026

    First General Meeting

    The first General Meeting on the draft Constitution was held, at which members considered the draft.

  3. 1 November 2026

    First General Convention

    The Constitution was adopted by the members at the First General Convention, and the first Executive Committee was elected.
