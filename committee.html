Executive Committee

The Executive Committee is responsible for the governance, administration, coordination and delivery of the Society’s activities between meetings of the General Assembly, and is accountable to the General Assembly.

First Executive Committee · elected 1 November 2026 · term ends at the AGM held in 2029

The Executive Committee works through collective leadership, shared responsibility and decision by Consensus. No officer has greater voting power because of a senior position, and no officer has a casting vote Article 6.5.
