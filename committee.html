Executive Volunteer Committee

Elected by our members at the First General Convention on 1 November 2026, the Executive Volunteer Committee leads the Society’s work on behalf of the whole community.

First Executive Volunteer Committee · 2026 – 2029

Our Executive Volunteer Committee is made up of volunteers who give their time to serve the community. They work as a team, make decisions collectively and are accountable to the members who elected them. The Committee will serve until the Annual General Meeting in 2029.
