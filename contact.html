Who handles your enquiry

The General Secretary coordinates official correspondence and administrative procedures Article 7.9.

  • Membership — Vice Chair Coordinator, Membership and Records
  • Programmes and events — Vice Chair Coordinator, Programmes and Community Engagement
  • Donations and sponsorship — Vice Chair Coordinator, Fundraising and Resource Development
  • Partnerships — Vice Chair Coordinator, Partnerships and External Relations
  • Equality and inclusion — Vice Chair Coordinator, Equality, Diversity and Inclusion
  • Formal complaints — in writing to the General Secretary or, where the complaint concerns the General Secretary, to the Chair Article 12.3

Only persons authorised by the Executive Committee may make commitments or statements on behalf of the Society Article 14.7.
